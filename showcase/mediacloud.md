# Product Management at MediaCloud

Leading product development for an open-source media analysis platform used by journalists, researchers, and academics worldwide.

## Key Achievements

- **User Research & Strategy**: Conducted extensive user interviews to understand journalist workflows and pain points
- **Feature Development**: Led development of new search and analysis tools that improved user engagement by 40%
- **Open Source Community**: Managed contributions from developers across 15+ countries
- **Technical Integration**: Worked closely with engineering teams to ensure scalable, maintainable solutions

## Impact

MediaCloud now processes over 1 billion articles annually and serves thousands of users in the journalism and research communities. The platform has been cited in academic papers and used by major news organizations for media analysis.
