Tah-dah! My attempt at a scrappy custom personal webpage, using Jekyll as a backend to compile a lightweight psudo-blog from git. 

This was an interesting lesson in vibe-coding. I knew how I wanted this page to look, and had some old parts lying around I wanted to use- but I had never used Jekyll and wanted to iterate fast in some spare time. I was able to use Cusor to spin up the layout I had drawn in my notebook very quickly, and to get some of the core interactions going. Cursor's attempts to architect both filtering and pagination in the blog feed with jekyll contradicted each other, so I had to step in to provide the actual architecture that ended up working. Vibe coding is very much not a hands-off process, as others have said!

The wavy lines to the right are repurposed from a very old project, from back during the Covid lockdown- I was excited to get to feature them in a personal project. 

The next steps for me are to actually start writing posts here! Writing my thoughts out for public consumption is one of those old-school habits I never developed, so I'm eager to start!
